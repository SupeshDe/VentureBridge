1. TITLE & PROJECT IDENTITY
================================================================================
Title       : VentureBridge - Smart Investor Collaboration Platform
Type        : Web-based discovery, matching and collaboration platform
Domain      : Startup / Investment Technology (FinTech-adjacent, no money movement)
Stack       : MERN (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) + Socket.IO
Version     : 1.0.0
Tagline     : "Stop pitching strangers. Start every call at 80%."


================================================================================
2. ABSTRACT
================================================================================
Early-stage founders waste months cold-emailing investors whose thesis they do
not fit, while investors drown in unscreened pitch decks. VentureBridge is a
web platform that connects startups/entrepreneurs with suitable investors
through a transparent, rule-based Smart Matching engine. Startups register and
describe their raise; investors describe their thesis; the system scores every
pair 0-100 across six weighted factors and explains each score. Matched parties
connect through collaboration requests, real-time chat with document sharing,
shared data rooms and scheduled calls. A three-role model (Entrepreneur,
Investor, Admin) governs the platform, with admin verification, content
moderation, reports handling and analytics. The system deliberately handles no
money movement - it is a discovery and collaboration layer only.


================================================================================
3. INTRODUCTION
================================================================================
Fundraising is a matching problem. A founder raising Rs. 20 lakh for an early-
stage healthcare startup gains nothing from a Series-B SaaS fund, yet today's
discovery channels (cold email, demo days, introduction chains) surface such
mismatches constantly. Investors face the mirror problem: high inbound volume
with low signal.

VentureBridge treats founder-investor fit as computable. Both sides declare
structured data (sector, stage, cheque size, geography, risk, equity), the
platform scores compatibility deterministically, and conversation starts only
on mutual interest. Trust is enforced through admin verification of accounts
and profiles, a reporting/moderation loop, and two-way star ratings that are
only available to connected counterparties.


================================================================================
4. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================
1. Founders cannot efficiently find investors whose thesis matches their raise.
2. Investors cannot filter inbound startups by thesis fit.
3. Existing match claims are black boxes - no explanation of WHY a score was given.
4. Introductions lack context (no shared decks, data rooms, or scheduled calls).
5. Platforms mix discovery with transactions, creating compliance risk.
6. Fake profiles and spam erode trust without verification and moderation.


================================================================================
5. OBJECTIVES
================================================================================
O1. Let entrepreneurs register, build rich startup profiles and declare funding
    requirements (amount, equity, stage, sector, location, risk, business model,
    revenue, team, documents).
O2. Let investors register, declare investment thesis (range, sectors, stages,
    locations, risk, equity band) and discover startups.
O3. Implement a transparent Smart Matching engine producing a 0-100 Match Score
    with per-factor explanations.
O4. Provide faceted search and filters on both sides, including minimum match
    score, minimum rating and Fit/rating sorting.
O5. Implement the collaboration flow: Send Request -> Accept -> Connection ->
    Chat (text + document attachments), plus data rooms and call scheduling.
O6. Provide role dashboards (founder, investor, admin) with actionable metrics.
O7. Provide admin governance: verification queues, user management (including
    safe deletion with full data cascade), industries, content moderation,
    reports queue, platform analytics.
O8. Notify users in-app and by email for every important event.
O9. Add AI-style assistance: heuristic startup analysis, match explanations,
    and a Gemini-powered assistant chat.
O10. Exclude all money movement; remain a discovery/collaboration layer.

6. SCOPE
================================================================================
IN SCOPE
- Registration, login (JWT httpOnly cookies), role-based access.
- Startup & investor profiles (create/read/update/delete).
- Document vault with uploads, visibility rules, pitch-deck linking.
- Smart matching, match scores, explanations, recommendations.
- Search, filters, min-score / min-rating filters, sorting.
- Collaboration requests (send/accept/reject/cancel), connections.
- Real-time chat (Socket.IO) with document attachments.
- Data rooms per connection; meeting slot proposals with accept/decline.
- Express interest (lightweight pre-request signal) + favourites/watchlist.
- Two-way star reviews restricted to connected parties.
- Reporting/moderation loop (user reports -> admin queue -> resolve).
- In-app notifications + transactional emails (welcome, requests, verification,
  matches, interest, meetings, reviews).
- Admin panel: overview analytics, users, startups, investors, verification,
  industries, content, reports.
- AI: heuristic startup analysis, Gemini assistant chat toggle.

OUT OF SCOPE (v1)
- Actual investment, payments, escrow, term sheets, cap-table management.
- Video calling (meeting links are external URLs).
- Mobile native apps (responsive web only).
- Automated KYC/identity document OCR (verification is human-reviewed).


================================================================================
7. LITERATURE REVIEW & EXISTING SYSTEMS
================================================================================
Existing channels and their gaps:
- Cold email / LinkedIn outreach: unfiltered, low response, no fit signal.
- Demo days / pitch events: geography-bound, infrequent, small samples.
- Angel networks (e.g. AngelList-style listings): listings without explainable
  fit scoring; diligence tooling is external.
- Generic freelance/marketplace matching: optimizes for transactions, not
  high-trust introduction workflows.

VentureBridge differentiates on: (a) deterministic, explainable scoring rather
than black-box recommendations; (b) verification + moderation + connection-
gated reviews as a trust stack; (c) diligence tooling (vault, data rooms,
scheduled calls) inside the introduction workflow; (d) explicit non-custodial
scope (no money movement).

8. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================
8.1 FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
Entrepreneur:
  FR-E1  Register, login, logout; account activates after admin verification.
  FR-E2  Create/edit/delete one startup profile: name, tagline, description,
         logo URL, website, industry, stage, location, risk, business model,
         target market, revenue, funding amount, valuation, equity offered,
         team size, founded year, tags, pitch-deck link, visibility.
  FR-E3  Upload documents (PDF/DOC/PPT/PNG/JPG, 25 MB) with
         private/connections/public visibility; link decks to the profile.
  FR-E4  Search/filter investors (industry, stage, location, risk, range,
         min match score, min rating; sort by fit/rating).
  FR-E5  View investor profiles, match scores and explanations.
  FR-E6  Send/track/cancel collaboration requests; accept flow creates a
         connection + chat thread.
  FR-E7  Chat with attachments; share data-room files; propose/accept calls.
  FR-E8  View AI profile insights (completeness, strengths, gaps, risks).
  FR-E9  Receive investor interest; view own inbound ratings.
  FR-E10 Rate connected investors (1-5 stars + note).

Investor:
  FR-I1  Register/login (same gate as above).
  FR-I2  Create/edit investor profile: organization, bio, location, preferred
         industries/stages/locations, risk, investment type, investment range,
         equity band.
  FR-I3  Discover startups with the same filter set; view full profiles,
         pitch decks and shared documents.
  FR-I4  Receive recommended startups with match scores + explanations.
  FR-I5  Express interest (lightweight signal) or send a full request;
         accept/reject inbound requests.
  FR-I6  Favourites/watchlist; withdraw interest.
  FR-I7  Chat, data rooms, call scheduling (same as founders).
  FR-I8  Rate connected founders; view own inbound ratings.

Admin:
  FR-A1  Platform overview analytics (users, roles, messages, documents,
         verification funnel, request pipeline, 7-day signup chart, system).
  FR-A2  Manage users: verify/unverify, activate/deactivate, inspect, delete
         (blocked for self and admins; full data cascade).
  FR-A3  Verify startups and investor profiles (approval triggers new-match
         alerts to the opposite side).
  FR-A4  Manage industries/categories (CRUD, activate/deactivate, delete
         blocked while in use, usage counts).
  FR-A5  Moderate uploaded content (list/filter/delete any file).
  FR-A6  Handle reports queue (pending/reviewing/resolved/dismissed).
  FR-A7  Any user can report any profile (spam, fraud, inappropriate,
         fake-profile, harassment, other).

8.2 NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
  NFR1 Performance: paginated lists (default 10-12, max 50); matching pools
       capped at 500 candidates; charts aggregate in-DB where possible.
  NFR2 Security: bcrypt (12 rounds), JWT httpOnly cookies, role middleware,
       rate limiting (300/15 min global, stricter auth limiter), Helmet,
       CORS allowlist, mongo-sanitize, HPP, zod validation, noCache.
  NFR3 Reliability: graceful shutdown, Mongo retry logging, email fire-and-
       forget (mail failures never break API requests), request IDs.
  NFR4 Usability: responsive layouts, ≤10-step type scale, ≤5 button variants,
       ARIA roles/labels on interactive controls, keyboard-operable dialogs.
  NFR5 Maintainability: layered backend (routes/controllers/services/models),
       centralized API client, seed scripts, Docker + CI configs.

9.3 USER ROLES & ACCESS CONTROL
  Roles: entrepreneur, investor, admin (User.role).
  - Public: landing, login, register.
  - Authenticated: discovery, requests, connections, chat, documents,
    notifications, settings, AI chat.
  - Entrepreneur-only: /startup/profile, /discover/investors, interests/received.
  - Investor-only: /investor/profile, /discover/startups, favourites,
    interests/me.
  - Admin-only: /admin/* (dashboard, users, startups, investors,
    verification, industries, content, reports).
  Enforcement: JWT (httpOnly cookie, 7d) -> protect middleware ->
  allowRoles(...) per route, on both page routes (frontend guards) and API
  routes (authoritative). Admin accounts are created only via seed script,
  never by public registration. Accounts start unverified; login is blocked
  with 403 until an admin verifies.

9.6 SMART MATCHING ALGORITHM
  Type: deterministic rule-based weighted scoring (transparent, auditable).
  Inputs: one Startup + one InvestorProfile. Output: score 0-100 + per-factor
  pass/fail + one-line natural-language explanation.
  Weights (total 100):
    Industry Match .............. 30
    Investment Range Match ...... 25   (fundingRequired.amount within
                                        investmentRange.minimum-maximum)
    Startup Stage Match ......... 15   (startupStage in preferredStages)
    Risk Preference Match ....... 10   (exact match, case-insensitive)
    Location Match .............. 10   (startup location in preferredLocations
                                        OR equals investor location)
    Equity Compatibility ........ 10   (equityOffered within equityMin-
                                        equityMax; null ask counts as pass)
  Worked example (matches the project demo data):
    Startup: Logistics / ask Rs.2 Cr / Seed / Medium risk / Bengaluru /
             12% equity.
    Investor: Logistics / Rs.50L-5Cr / Seed / Medium / Bengaluru / 5-20%.
    30+25+15+10+10+10 = 100 -> "Strong fit at 100%. Aligned on Industry,
    Investment Range, Startup Stage, Risk Preference, Location, Equity."
  Verdicts: >=75 Strong fit; 50-74 Worth a conversation; <50 Weak fit.
  Served two ways: dedicated /matching/* endpoints and inline on discovery
  lists (?minScore=&sort=matchScore|-matchScore, pool capped at 500).

9.7 CORE WORKFLOWS
  W1 Registration: register (201, pendingVerification) -> admin verifies
     (in-app + email) -> login (httpOnly JWT cookie).
  W2 Discovery: filters (+minScore/minRating/sort) -> score pills with
     explanations -> detail modal (facts, diligence files, rating, report).
  W3 Collaboration: Send Request -> receiver Accepts -> Connection +
     Conversation created -> Chat (text + attachments) -> data room files,
     call proposals, reviews.
  W4 Interest (light path): investor expresses interest -> founder notified
     (in-app + email) -> founder requests when warm.
  W5 Moderation: any user reports profile -> admin queue -> reviewing ->
     resolved/dismissed (+ enforce via Users page).
  W6 Verification: admin approves startup/investor -> owner notified AND
     top opposite-side matches auto-alerted (capped: 3 investors / 5 founders,
     score >= 60).

11. SECURITY IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================
- Passwords: bcrypt, 12 rounds, never selected by default.
- Sessions: JWT in httpOnly, SameSite=lax cookies (secure in production).
- Authorization: protect + allowRoles on every non-public route; frontend
  guards mirror but never replace backend checks.
- Abuse: global + auth rate limiters; safe user deletion (self/admin
  deletion blocked; cascade removes startups, profiles, requests,
  connections, conversations, messages, favourites, interests, meetings
  artefacts, documents + stored files, reviews, notifications).
- Input: zod schemas (strip unknown keys), mongo-sanitize, HPP, file type +
  size allowlists, Helmet headers, CORS allowlist, request-id tracing.


================================================================================
12. NOTIFICATION & EMAIL SUBSYSTEM
================================================================================
Every key event creates an in-app notification (bell + unread counts) and,
when SMTP is configured, a themed HTML+text email (fire-and-forget; mail
failures never break API requests; full no-op with debug log otherwise).
Covered events: welcome (pending verification), request received/accepted/
declined, interest received, meeting proposed/accepted, verification
approved/rejected (account, startup, investor), new high-fit matches,
new reviews, new chat messages (in-app/socket). Mail links use a configured
public URL (CLIENT_URL_PUBLIC) so they open on phones, never localhost.

================================================================================
15. RESULTS & DISCUSSION
================================================================================
The platform delivers the full objective chain: Registration -> Profile ->
Funding Requirements -> Investor Preferences -> Smart Matching -> Match
Score (+ explanation) -> Collaboration Request -> Connection -> Chat, and
the reverse Investor flow. Verification-gated login plus connection-gated
reviews keep signal quality high without handling money. The deterministic
matcher proved more explainable (and thesis-appropriate) than a black-box
recommender; the main trade-off is no learning from behaviour (requires
interaction-event history - listed in future scope).


================================================================================
16. CONCLUSION
================================================================================
VentureBridge meets its objective: a secure, role-based, enterprise-
structured MERN platform where founders and investors discover each other by
computable fit, collaborate with real diligence tooling, and build trust
through verification, moderation and mutual ratings - with no financial
transactions in scope.
